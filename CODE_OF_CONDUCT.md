# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback about
work. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats and publishing another person's
private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content
or restrict participation, taking context and repeated behaviour into account.

Report conduct concerns privately through
[the repository's private reporting channel](https://github.com/intesarjawad/rowsetta/security/advisories/new).
Do not put private reports in an issue or pull request.
